# Avatar credits

Pre-rendered avatar styles are free alternatives to Synapse's own procedural
blob (see `docs/UI.md` §4). Licenses were checked on 2026-10-14 at
<https://dicebear.com/licenses>. The full machine-readable manifest is
`assets/avatars/manifest.json` (generated by `scripts/gen-avatars`).

## DiceBear styles (code: dicebear/dicebear, MIT)

Rendered with the DiceBear CLI at build time (`scripts/gen-avatars`, dev-only
via npx; never at runtime) from a seed + options into
`assets/avatars/<style>/` as small SVGs.

### CC0 (no attribution required)

Notionists, Lorelei, Open Peeps, Thumbs, Shapes, Moods, Critters, Pixel Art,
Pixelbot, Voxel Bot, Glass, Rings, Marbles, Planets, Clay, Blobs — all CC0
(<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>), by DiceBear.

### Free for personal + commercial (Pablo Stanley)

Bottts, Avataaars — Pablo Stanley's free license, via DiceBear
(<https://dicebear.com/licenses>).

### CC BY 4.0 (attribution required, credited here)

Fun Emoji, Adventurer, Big Smile, Croodles, Micah, Personas, Toon Head —
CC BY 4.0 (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>), by DiceBear.
If you publish an avatar rendered from these styles, keep this credit.

## Role badges (Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D, MIT)

Researcher 🔬, Critic ⚖️, Writer ✍️, Lead ⭐ — MIT
(<https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji/blob/main/LICENSE>),
by Microsoft. Drawn on top of any avatar as a small corner badge.

## Not bundled

Google Noto animated emoji (CC BY 4.0) is not bundled: animated WebP/GIF
playback is not confirmed in our pinned GPUI version. Multiavatar (custom
license, unmaintained) and Lottie/Rive packs (no GPUI player) are rejected.

## Our own blob

The default green-bean blob is drawn procedurally by Synapse
(`synapse-app::avatar`) — no third-party art, no attribution needed.
