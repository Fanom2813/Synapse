# Research method

Use this skill when the team runs deep research.

1. Turn the goal into 3-8 research questions.
2. Gather sources from at least two channels; prefer primary sources.
3. Record URL, title and the exact quote for every claim.
4. Report findings with citations and confidence; list open questions.
